# Query: HP 24yh

The HP 24yh is a 23.8-inch Full HD LED-backlit monitor designed for clear visuals and a user-friendly experience. It features an IPS panel for wide viewing angles and consistent image accuracy.

### Specifications

*   **Display Size:** 23.8 inches (diagonal)
*   **Panel Type:** IPS (In-Plane Switching) with W-LED backlight
*   **Resolution:** Full HD (1920 x 1080) at 60 Hz
*   **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
*   **Brightness:** 250 cd/m² (nits)
*   **Contrast Ratio:**
    *   Static: 1000:1
    *   Dynamic: 10,000,000:1
*   **Response Time:** 5 ms (gray to gray)
*   **Viewing Angles:** 178° horizontal and 178° vertical
*   **Color Support:** Up to 16.7 million colors
*   **Connectivity:**
    *   1 x VGA
    *   1 x DVI-D
    *   1 x HDMI
*   **Features:**
    *   Anti-glare screen
    *   Low Blue Light mode
    *   VESA mount compatible (100 x 100 mm)
    *   Tilt adjustable (-5° to +20°)
    *   HDCP support
    *   Energy Star certified
    *   EPEAT Silver registered
*   **Dimensions (with stand):** 21.52 x 8.26 x 15.95 inches (W x D x H) / 54.66 x 20.99 x 40.51 cm (W x D x H)
*   **Weight (with stand):** 3.65 kg (8.05 lbs) / 10.6 pounds
*   **Model Number:** 3UA73AA#ABA
