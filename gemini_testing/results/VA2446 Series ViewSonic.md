# Query: VA2446 Series ViewSonic

The ViewSonic VA2446 Series includes monitors designed for home and office use, featuring LED backlights and Full HD resolution. Two prominent models in this series are the VA2446m-LED and the VA2446mh-LED, which share many specifications but differ in connectivity and dynamic contrast ratio.

### ViewSonic VA2446 Series Specifications

Here are the detailed specifications for the ViewSonic VA2446m-LED and VA2446mh-LED models:

**Display**
*   **Size:** 24 inches (23.6 inches viewable)
*   **Panel Type:**
    *   VA2446m-LED: TN
    *   VA2446mh-LED: MVA
*   **Backlight:** LED
*   **Resolution:** Full HD 1920 x 1080
*   **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
*   **Brightness:** 300 cd/m² (typical)
*   **Static Contrast Ratio:**
    *   VA2446m-LED: 1,000:1 (typical)
    *   VA2446mh-LED: 3,000:1
*   **Dynamic Contrast Ratio:**
    *   VA2446m-LED: 10,000,000:1 (MEGA Dynamic Contrast Ratio)
    *   VA2446mh-LED: 50,000,000:1 (MEGA Dynamic Contrast Ratio)
*   **Response Time:** 5ms (typical)
*   **Viewing Angles (H/V):**
    *   VA2446m-LED: 170°/160°
    *   VA2446mh-LED: 178°/178°
*   **Colors:** 16.7 Million
*   **Panel Surface:** Anti-glare, Hard Coating (3H)
*   **Refresh Rate:**
    *   VA2446m-LED: 50 Hz - 75 Hz
    *   VA2446mh-LED: Not explicitly stated, but typically 60Hz for 5ms monitors. (One source mentions 60Hz, and another 75Hz for the VA2446M-LED model)
*   **Backlight Life:**
    *   VA2446m-LED: 30,000 hours (minimum)
    *   VA2446mh-LED: 40,000 hours (minimum)

**Connectivity**
*   **VA2446m-LED:**
    *   VGA x 1
    *   DVI x 1 (with HDCP)
    *   3.5mm Audio In x 1
*   **VA2446mh-LED:**
    *   VGA x 1
    *   HDMI 1.4 In x 1
    *   3.5mm Audio In x 1
    *   3.5mm Audio Out x 1

**Audio**
*   **Built-in Speakers:** Yes (integrated speakers)
    *   VA2446mh-LED: 2.5W (x2)

**Power**
*   **Consumption (typical):**
    *   VA2446m-LED: 26W
    *   VA2446mh-LED: 26W
*   **Eco Mode:** Yes (Conserve and Optimize modes)
    *   VA2446m-LED: Optimize 18W, Conserve 15W
    *   VA2446mh-LED: Eco Mode (Optimized) 21W, Eco Mode (Conserve) 17W
*   **Stand-by:** 0.5W
*   **Voltage:** AC 100-240V (universal), 50-60Hz

**Ergonomics and Features**
*   **VESA Mountable:** Yes
*   **Kensington Lock Slot:** Yes
*   **OSD (On Screen Display):** Yes
*   **Certifications:** ENERGY STAR®, TCO, CE, CB, WEEE, RoHS, REACH SVHC List, UL, cUL, FCC-B / ICES-003B, TUV-S, Nemko ERGO, GOSTR+ Hygienic, UkrSEPRO, ErP, CCC, CEL Level 1, BSMI, PSB, C-tick, KC, e-standby

**Physical Dimensions (with stand)**
*   **VA2446m-LED:** 22.5” x 15.8” x 8.3” (W x H x D)
*   **Weight (VA2446m-LED):** 3.7 kg
