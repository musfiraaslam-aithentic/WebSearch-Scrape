Here are the specifications for the Sceptre F27 monitor:

**Sceptre F27 Monitor Specifications**

*   **Display Size:** 27 inches
*   **Panel Type:** IPS (In-Plane Switching)
*   **Resolution:** 1920 x 1080 (Full HD)
*   **Refresh Rate:**
    *   Up to 75Hz
    *   Some models may support up to 100Hz (depending on the specific F27 variant)
*   **Contrast Ratio:** 5,000,000:1 (High Contrast)
*   **Response Time:** Fast Response Time
*   **Brightness:** 320 cd/m²
*   **Color Gamut:** 99% sRGB coverage. Some reviews indicate 99.2% sRGB coverage and 113.2% sRGB volume.

    *   2 x HDMI (accelerates refresh rate up to 75Hz, or up to 100Hz depending on model)
    *   1 x VGA
    *   PC audio in/out
    *   Audio out/Headphone Jack
    *   Some models may also include a DisplayPort
*   **Speakers:** Built-in speakers
*   **Design:** Ultra-slim, Edgeless Design
*   **Special Features:**
    *   AMD FreeSync
    *   Blue Light Shift
    *   Anti-Flicker
    *   FPS-RTS Game Modes
    *   Tiltable Display
*   **VESA Mount Compatible:** Yes, 100mm x 100mm
*   **Color:** Machine Black / Ultra Slim Metal Black
