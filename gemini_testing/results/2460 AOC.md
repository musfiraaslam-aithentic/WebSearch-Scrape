# Query: 2460 AOC

The "2460 AOC" refers to a series of AOC monitors, with several distinct models such as the G2460PF, G2460PQU, G2460VQ6, and E2460SD, each offering varying specifications primarily aimed at different user needs, including gaming and general use.

### Specifications

**AOC G2460PF (Gaming Monitor)**
*   **Display Size:** 24 inches
*   **Panel Type:** TN (Twisted Nematic)
*   **Backlight:** W-LED
*   **Resolution:** 1920 x 1080 (Full HD)
*   **Refresh Rate:** 144Hz (variable, with Adaptive-Sync/FreeSync Premium)
*   **Response Time (GtG):** 1 ms
*   **Brightness:** 350 cd/m²
*   **Static Contrast Ratio:** 1000:1
*   **Dynamic Contrast Ratio:** 80,000,000:1
*   **Viewing Angles (H/V):** 170° / 160° (some sources mention 178° horizontal)
*   **Color Support:** 16.7 million (6-bits per subpixel plus dithering)
*   **Connectivity:**
    *   1 x USB 2.0 (upstream)
    *   4 x USB 2.0 (downstream) (one red for fast charging)
    *   1 x HDMI 1.4 (MHL)
    *   1 x DisplayPort 1.2 (Adaptive-Sync support)
    *   1 x DVI-D Dual Link
    *   1 x D-sub (VGA)
    *   1 x 3.5 mm Audio In
    *   1 x 3.5 mm Audio Out (headphone jack)
*   **Ergonomics:** Tilt, height adjustment (130mm), swivel (270°), pivot (90° clockwise)
*   **Power Consumption:** 22.7W (typical)
*   **Features:** AMD FreeSync Premium, Picture Boost, Eco Mode, e-Saver, i-Menu, Off Timer, Screen+ Software
