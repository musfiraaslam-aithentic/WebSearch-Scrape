# Query: ASUS VT229

The ASUS VT229H is a 21.5-inch Full HD (1920x1080) multi-touch monitor designed for an intuitive and interactive experience. It features an IPS panel for wide viewing angles and vibrant colors, and it is compliant with Windows 10/11 for optimal touch functionality.

### Specifications

*   **Display**
    *   **Panel Size:** 21.5 inches (54.6 cm)
    *   **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
    *   **Panel Type:** IPS (In-Plane Switching)
    *   **Resolution:** 1920 x 1080 (Full HD)
    *   **Brightness:** 250 cd/m²
    *   **Contrast Ratio (Static):** 1000:1
    *   **ASUS Smart Contrast Ratio (ASCR):** 100,000,000:1
    *   **Viewing Angles (H/V):** 178°/178°
    *   **Response Time:** 5ms (Gray to Gray)
    *   **Display Colors:** 16.7 Million (6-Bits + FRC)
    *   **Touch Screen:** 10-point multi-touch, Capacitive
    *   **Hardness:** 7H
    *   **Refresh Rate:** 50~75 Hz (Vertical)
    *   **Backlight:** W-LED
    *   **Surface:** Anti-Glare
    *   **Frameless Design:** Yes

*   **Audio**
    *   **Speakers:** 2 x 1.5W Stereo RMS

*   **I/O Ports**
    *   **HDMI:** 1x HDMI(v1.4)
    *   **VGA:** 1x D-Sub (VGA)
    *   **Audio Input:** 3.5mm Mini-Jack

*   **Power Consumption**
    *   **Power Consumption:** 18.56W
    *   **Power Saving Mode:** <0.5W

*   **Mechanical Design**
    *   **Tilt:** +20° ~ -5°
    *   **VESA Wall Mounting:** 100x100mm

*   **Features**
    *   **ASUS Eye Care Technology:** Flicker-free and Low Blue Light
    *   **Trace Free Technology:** Yes
    *   **SPLENDID Technology:** Yes
    *   **OS Compatibility:** Windows 11/10 compliant

*   **Dimensions & Weight (Estimated)**
    *   **Net Weight:** 3.7 kg (8.16 lbs)
